Shrimp Fried Rice

license info

Description:

I first tried this dish when my mom made it. It is spicy, shrimpy, and delightful!

Ingredients:

  • rice
  • shrimp
  • eggs
  • mushrooms
  • carrots
  • celery
  • onion
  • salt
  • sesame oil
  • fresh garlic
  • green curry paste

Steps:

  1. start your preferred method of cooking rice
  2. dice onions, slice mushrooms, and cut carrots and celery
  3. heat a large pot
  4. add onions, mushrooms, carrots, and celery to the pot
  5. add shrimp
  6. stir in eggs
  7. squeeze fresh garlic into pot
  8. add the cooked rice to the other ingredients and stir
  9. add salt, sesame oil, and green curry paste to taste
  10. enjoy!
