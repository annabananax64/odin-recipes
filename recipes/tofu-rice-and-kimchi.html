Tofu, Rice, and Kimchi

image by Charles Haynes on Flickr

license info

Description:

This dish is a vegetarian version of a dinner my parents frequently made when I was a kid - fish, rice, and kimchi. It is simple and delicious.

Ingredients:

  • rice
  • kimchi
  • extra firm tofu
  • cooking oil
  • soy sauce
  • sesame oil or butter
  • furikake

Steps:

  1. start the rice using whatever cooking method you prefer
  2. add a little cooking oil to the bottom of a pan
  3. start heating the pan
  4. cube half of the block of tofu and spread evenly in the pan
  5. drizzle the tofu with soy sauce
  6. tip the pan around to ensure all the tofu cubes make contact with the soy sauce
  7. cook the tofu for 5-10 minutes, depending on how high the heat is
  8. flip each cube of tofu and cook for 5-10 more minutes
  9. when the rice is done, add it to a bowl
  10. add either butter or sesame oil to the rice
  11. top rice with a little soy sauce and a lot of furikake
  12. add as much kimchi as you desire to the bowl
  13. add the tofu to the bowl
  14. enjoy!
